Nexus911 02 · Demo · What you'll see

Six ideas, each one visible on screen.

01 · Claim-level graph

Every statement keeps its caller and quote.

Robbery → What callers report: white and black both stay on screen, with sources.

02 · Quote-grounded guard

No quote, no fact.

An extracted fact without a verbatim span of the transcript is rejected.

03 · Information-gain priority

Repeats sink, new facts rise.

The 11th robbery caller says “knife”: 90 while repeats sit at 0–22.

04 · Independence-aware weighting

An echoed rumor can't outvote eyewitnesses.

Hearsay (“my neighbor said”) and a repeat caller count for less.

05 · Explainable three-band linking

Auto, suggest or new, with the reasons.

Knife call: auto at 0.89. Fire call: capped at 0.49 by the type gate, so new. No-town caller: suggest. One click to Confirm or Split.

06 · Massachusetts-aware

Old exits, duplicate streets, transfers.

Pike: 12 calls → 1 incident at Exit 63 (old Exit 8). Washington St: Boston vs. Brookline kept apart. Spanish and Portuguese callers link like any other.

No call is ever dropped. Evaluation over all three scenarios: 28 calls → 5 incidents (82% collapsed), 5/5 new incidents and 10/10 new-info calls caught. Synthetic data: it shows the design works, not real-world accuracy.
